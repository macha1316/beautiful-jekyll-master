Focus Now Privacy Policy

Focus Now (the “App”) places a high priority on protecting personal information so that users can manage tasks and time with confidence. This privacy policy outlines how we handle information within the App.

1. Information We Collect

  • Task entries and setting data entered in the App are stored on the user’s device.
  • Third-party services, such as ad networks, may collect advertising IDs and usage data to deliver ads or analyze how the App is used.

1.1 Purpose of Use

  • To deliver and improve the App’s features
  • To serve and optimize advertisements
  • To manage subscription status
  • To provide AI-powered assistance (we may send entered text to external AI services when necessary)

1.2 Data Storage

At present, task and setting data entered by the user are not stored in the cloud. If we add a cloud storage feature in the future, we will update this privacy policy and notify users in advance or at the time the feature is launched.

1.3 Disclosure to Third Parties

Except as required by law, we do not disclose user personal information to third parties.

1.4 Security

We implement reasonable safeguards to prevent unauthorized access and information leakage.

2. Third-Party Services

  • Ad delivery services: to display ads and analyze usage
  • RevenueCat: to manage subscriptions
  • OpenAI API: to provide AI assistance based on task content

3. Contact

For privacy inquiries, please reach out here.

4. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised without prior notice due to law changes or new features. Any updates will be published on this page.
